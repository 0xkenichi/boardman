## What this is

Boardman is the **cashier + fairness layer** for skill matches at your shop:

- Players stake **USDC** (they can fund with ₦ via Paystack)
- Both **lock** before play — no “pay me after” drama
- Final photo + both report → **auto settle**
- Works for **console, mobile, and table games** (Chess, Ludo, Monopoly)

You keep the room, the TVs, and the crowd. We handle money and disputes.

## What you earn

About **1.5% of matched stake volume** from Boardman’s fee — **not** an extra fee on players.

Example: two players lock $5 each → $10 matched → you earn **$0.15**.

Payouts: weekly USDC (or as agreed).
