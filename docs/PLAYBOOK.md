## How to earn PLAY

| Action | Base | Notes |
|--------|------|--------|
| **Win** | +100 | × streak × chain × rival |
| **Loss** (honest) | +40 | Same mults (no streak) |
| **Draw** | +50 | Both |
| **No-show** | **−50** | Never rewarded |
| Stake bonus | up to +50 | ~5 per $1 USDC stake |

### Chain mult (testnet mission)

| Chain | Mult |
|-------|------|
| **Arc** | **1.50×** (best — we need Arc volume) |
| **Avalanche** | **1.25×** |
| **Base** | **1.00×** |

### New players > same friend loop

| Who you play | Mult |
|--------------|------|
| Brand-new rival (first settle together) | **1.45×** |
| Their **first ever** Rematch settle | × **1.25** extra |
| Regular rematch | still earn (**~0.90×** floor) |

Bring new Telegram users onto testnet. Rematching friends is fine — recruiting pays more.

### Hot streak (wins)

Up to ~2.5× at 10-win streak. Loss / no-show resets streak.

### Multi-chain

Settle on **Arc + Avalanche + Base** in a season for airdrop **weight** bonus (see airdrop doc).
