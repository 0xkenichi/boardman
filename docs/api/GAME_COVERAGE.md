# sideQuest Game Coverage

Our goal is to support the most popular competitive titles with high-accuracy automated score verification. We are starting with the following "Big Three" sports titles and will expand based on user demand.

## 1. EA Sports FC (Formerly FIFA)
*   **Supported Platforms**: PS5, Xbox Series X/S, PC.
*   **Match Types**: 
    *   1v1 Kick-off
    *   Ultimate Team (UT) Friendly Matches
*   **Verification**: Screenshots of the final whistle screen showing the score and PSN/Xbox IDs.

## 2. Madden NFL
*   **Supported Platforms**: PS5, Xbox Series X/S.
*   **Match Types**: 
    *   1v1 Online Head-to-Head
    *   Madden Ultimate Team (MUT)
*   **Verification**: Final score summary screen.

## 3. NBA 2K
*   **Supported Platforms**: PS5, Xbox Series X/S.
*   **Match Types**: 
    *   Play Now Online
    *   MyPark / MyTeam 1v1
*   **Verification**: Post-game box score screen.
